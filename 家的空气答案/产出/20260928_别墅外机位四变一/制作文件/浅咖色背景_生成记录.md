# 浅咖色纯背景

使用内置 imagegen 生成，未使用CLI。画布为竖版9:16方向。输出为项目根目录“浅咖色纯背景_竖版.png”，用于放在透明UI动画下方。

## 最终提示词

Use case: productivity-visual. Generate a plain full-bleed background image for overlaying a fine-line architectural UI animation. Portrait aspect ratio 9:16, ideally 2160x3840. Entire canvas is a single uniform very light warm coffee / pale cafe-au-lait beige color, approximately #E9DFD2. Flat solid color edge to edge, clean understated light taupe-beige, not dark brown, not yellow or gold, not pink, no blue. Absolutely empty background only. No objects, architecture, illustration, text, letters, logo, border, frame, pattern, paper grain, fabric, shadows, light beams, horizon, vignette, or gradient. Keep brightness and color uniform across the entire image.

尺寸与颜色描述为生成意图，不将模型原始输出宣称为精确色值或原生4K。
